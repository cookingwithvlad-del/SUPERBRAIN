Your house rules

Pulled straight from the corrections you made this year. Every skill below enforces these.

  1. Never invent facts about you. The "former Alinea sous chef" ad is the reason. Credentials come only from your verified profile. No made-up metrics, views, income or endorsements.

  2. Change only what you asked. "Use these photos but don't change them except colors." Faces and likeness stay exactly as they are.

  3. Deliver usable files. Separate files, not zips. Right size, right format, stated up front.

  4. Build on what worked. "7 types of chefs, which one are you" had the best traction. Ask Claude for more of a proven format instead of "make it viral".

  5. Respect the people at the table. Some guests stay unnamed by choice (the rabbi, the hospice, WNRS makers, Ep 49's kitchen). Check before naming anyone.

And the brief you'd save, the one that fixes most problems:

WHAT

What are we making? A 30s reel, a recipe page, a pitch slide.

WHO

Who is it for? Followers, investors, a private client.

KEEP TRUE

Facts, faces, names and details that can't change.

DELIVER

Format, size, length, separate files. Then check against the brief.

Why so many things "fail to connect"

The long list of red errors comes from the plugin bundles loaded in your Claude Code cloud sessions (Marketing, Small Business, Engineering, Finance, Data and more). Each bundle ships its own connectors, like HubSpot, PagerDuty, Datadog and Honeycomb. The cloud session's network blocks those hosts, so they error. Your real accounts are fine. Most of them are tools you'll never use, so the fix is to remove the bundles, not to connect them.
