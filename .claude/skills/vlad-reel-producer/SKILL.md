# Vlad reel producer

Standing workflow: every piece of footage Vlad shoots becomes professional short-form reels. Apply the vlad-brief house rules (verified facts only, no invented metrics, separate files).

## Style
- Pace: Joshua Weissman / Nick DiGiovanni. Hard cuts, sizzle and texture close-ups, sound-driven edits, no dead air.
- Story: Anthony Bourdain. Who is this person, why this dish, what changed at the table.
- Hook in the first 1-2 seconds, no logo open. One idea per reel.
- Default reel: 9:16, ~30s, 15-22 shots. Main episodes: 16:9, 8-10 min, each yielding three standalone reels.
- Emotional Chef on the House reels may end on: "We came in as strangers. We left after dinner."

## Steps
1. Get the footage. Ask for a direct download link if upload fails (Dropbox dl=1, Drive direct link).
2. Transcribe and find moments: the hook line, the reveal (plating, first bite, the guest's reaction), the story beat.
3. Write 3 hook options (on-screen text + first spoken line). Pick one and say why.
4. Pick the tool:
   - Descript: long footage, transcript-based trimming, filler removal, captions, full episodes.
   - OpusClip: many clips from one long video, social copy, scheduling.
   - AI Producer by OpusClip: one quick captioned rough cut.
   - Epidemic Sound: music and SFX (licensed only).
   - Canva: thumbnails and carousel versions.
5. Edit: hook → context → cook → reveal → payoff/question. Music drops on the payoff.
6. Deliver separately: captioned MP4, clean MP4, SRT, thumbnail, plus captions per platform (IG @chef_briantsev, TikTok @cookingwithvlad, YouTube Shorts title). Share/download links shown in full.
7. Check: hook in first 2s, nothing unverified on screen, guest privacy respected, files separate.
