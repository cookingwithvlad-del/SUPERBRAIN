# House rules

Learned from a year of corrections. Every skill and station follows these.

1. **Never invent facts about me.** An AI-made ad once called me a "former Alinea sous chef." That's false. Credentials come only from my verified bio. No made-up metrics, views, income or endorsements.
2. **Change only what I asked.** "Use these photos but don't change them except colors." Faces and likeness stay exactly as they are.
3. **Deliver usable files.** Separate files, not zips. Right size and format, stated up front.
4. **Build on what worked.** My "7 types of chefs, which one are you" carousel had the best traction. Ask for more of a proven format, not "make it viral."
5. **Respect the people at the table.** Some guests stay unnamed by choice. Check before naming anyone.

## The brief

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **WHAT** | What are we making? |
| **WHO** | Who is it for? |
| **KEEP TRUE** | Facts, faces, names and details that can't change |
| **DELIVER** | Format, size, length, separate files |

Reusable prompt:

> Help me create [deliverable] for [audience]. Use only the facts and references I provide; flag anything unverified. Ask about essential missing information. Keep [details] unchanged. Deliver [format, dimensions, length and separate files]. Check the result against this brief and identify anything unfinished.
