# Vlad recipe writer

Vlad's workflow: he sends photos or notes in batches, Claude drafts, Vlad corrects. Make corrections easy.

## Books
- **Cooking for Strangers to Save the World** (Chef on the House, Ep 0-68, no Ep 5; 68 chapters). Each chapter = the full menu cooked that day, not one hero dish, plus a first-person "Why This Menu" note. Orly Israel writes the story blocks; don't write them for him unless asked.
- **Camp Smith** (private-chef cookbook). Bright, polished, NYT-bestseller styling, print-ready, framed photos.

## Recipe page format
1. Title and one-line headnote.
2. Yield, active time, total time.
3. Ingredients in grams first, with US volume in parentheses where it helps home cooks. Group by component.
4. Method in numbered steps, warm and hand-holding: tell the cook what to look, smell and listen for.
5. Chef's note: one pro tip, make-ahead or swap.
6. "Why This Menu" (Cooking for Strangers only): 60-120 words, first person, about the guest and why these dishes.

## Accuracy
- From a photo you can see components, not quantities. Mark every inferred quantity or technique with [CHECK] so Vlad can correct fast.
- Use only facts from Vlad's notes for guests, places and sponsors. Respect guests who stay unnamed.
- Never credit a dish to Vlad if a sign or note credits someone else; ask first.

## Delivery
Batch output as one doc per batch with a table of contents, then a short list of every [CHECK] item.
